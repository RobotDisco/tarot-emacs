* [0.1.0] - 2026-07-16

** Added

- Shuffling and drawing from a standard 78-card deck, with random
  upright/reversed orientation assigned at draw time.
- Preset "Single Card", "Three Card", and "Celtic Cross" spreads, with
  =tarot-spreads= customizable to define your own.
- Card meanings for all 78 cards, upright and reversed.
- =tarot-reading= interactive command, opening a dedicated buffer that
  displays each spread position, its card, and orientation.
- =n=/=p= keybindings to move focus between positions in the reading
  buffer, showing the focused card's meaning; =q= quits via inherited
  =special-mode= bindings.
- Customization group and faces for spread positions, major/minor
  arcana names, and card orientation.
- =tarot-show-reversed-orientation= option to suppress reversed display
  and always show cards upright.
- Support for Emacs 26.1 and newer.
- Autoloaded =tarot-reading= entry point.
